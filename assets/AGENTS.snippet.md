## Persistent project knowledge

- Before substantial work, read `PROJECT_MEMORY.md`, `{{KNOWLEDGE_DIR}}/current-state.md`, and `{{KNOWLEDGE_DIR}}/deferred-work.md`. When debugging, also read `{{KNOWLEDGE_DIR}}/debugging-pitfalls.md`.
- Treat `.agents/skills/project-knowledge-base/SKILL.md` as the maintenance SOP. Load decisions, workflows, open questions, and diagnostics only when relevant; do not ingest the entire evidence tree.
- Update project memory after material facts, experiments, decisions, root causes, deployments, rollbacks, blockers, or supersessions, and before finalizing a materially changed task.
- Put anything impossible to determine or complete now—but mandatory later—into `deferred-work.md` with an unblock condition, future verification steps, and required acceptance evidence. Never silently drop or prematurely resolve it.
- Record reusable debugging traps in `debugging-pitfalls.md`, including symptom, root cause, misleading signals, proven fix, prevention gate, scope, and evidence.
- Keep entry documents concise, distinguish verified facts from hypotheses, preserve failed evidence, and mark stale conclusions superseded instead of deleting history.
